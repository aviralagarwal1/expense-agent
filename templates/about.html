Aviral Agarwal

I built Compline because I carry more than one credit card, and each one comes with its own app and its own list of charges. None of them shows the whole picture, and I didn’t want to connect a bank account just to see it.

Compline sits between typing every charge into a spreadsheet and importing everything automatically. You screenshot the charges your card apps already show you, Compline reads them, and nothing is saved to your history until you confirm it. If a charge looks like one you’ve already saved, Compline flags it so the same purchase isn’t counted twice.

AI can read a screenshot and catch a likely repeat on its own. But it never needs access to your personal accounts, and you stay in the loop on what counts as your spending.
