Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

Free. We provide a daily allowance of screenshots that is sufficient for most users.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. There is nothing to link and no credentials to hand over. You upload screenshots from apps you are already signed into.

Which cards does it work with?

Any card whose app shows a list of charges. You save your own card labels by adding the company and reference digits.

What if a charge is read wrong?

Correct the merchant, date, or amount before you add it. Nothing enters your history until you say so, and you can still edit or delete a row afterwards.

How does it avoid double counting?

Exact repeats are skipped automatically. Close matches are left for you to decide.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export the charges you’re viewing to CSV, print your history to PDF, or create a PowerPoint summary.
